Why Forever Free?

The early internet had a generous streak.

People made software because they had an idea, solved a problem, or simply wanted to share something useful. It didn’t need an account, harvest your data, or interrupt you with an ad every thirty seconds.

I want to keep some of that spirit alive. Everything here is free to use, free of ads, and built to respect your time and privacy. If a project can work without collecting your data, it will.

These projects are personal, sometimes a little unusual, and always made with care. I hope one of them makes your day easier—or at least more fun.
