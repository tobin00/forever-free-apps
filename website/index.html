Why Forever Free?

The early internet had a generous streak.

People made software because they had an idea, solved a problem, or simply wanted to share something useful. It didn't interrupt you with an ad every thirty seconds.

I want to keep some of that spirit alive. Everything here is free to use, free of ads, and built to respect your time and privacy. And I don't track you or collect your personal data.

These projects solve personal problems, reproduce functionality I found somewhere else that was full of ads, or I just find them amusing. I hope one of them makes your day easier, or at least more fun.
